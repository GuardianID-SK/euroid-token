Project overview

Identity should be verifiable without becoming intrusive.

EuroID is positioned as an experimental technology layer around digital identity, authentication and trusted transactions. The project combines blockchain transparency with the broader cybersecurity and identity-protection work of Guardian ID.

◈

Identity primitives

Explore reusable building blocks for attestations, proof references, account-level reputation and verifiable digital interactions.

⌁

Privacy-aware design

Favor selective disclosure, minimized data exposure and architectures that avoid putting sensitive personal data directly on-chain.

◎

Open verification

Use public blockchain infrastructure where transparency is useful, while keeping identity data and operational controls appropriately separated.
